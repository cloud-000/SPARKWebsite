Get involved

Volunteer

Help us run engaging STEM clubs for kids in local schools. Whether you are a student volunteer or a parent chaperone, there is a place for you.

We want you to join us

Everyone can make a difference, and we need all the help we can get. Choose a path below to see how you can participate.

*You must be a high school student to volunteer with SPARK.

Step 1: Find the chapter you want to join

Visit the Chapters page to see the list of existing chapters. Find a location that works for you with lesson subjects that fits your interest.

Step 2: Reach out

Contact us and express your interest in joining the chapter.

And you are done! We will be in touch with you shortly.

*You can only miss up to two sessions per season unless there is an unforeseen circumstance. Please tell your local club leader if you need to miss a session.

Step 1: Contact us

Contact us and express your interest in starting a chapter. We can provide help and our experiences along the way to ensure your success.

Step 2: Gather some friends

Find other people who are also interested and willing to help you start the chapter. We recommend at least having 4-5 founding members.

Step 3: Come up with a plan

Brainstorm what you want to teach and how you will teach it. Come up with a basic curriculum.

Step 4: Reach out to your local schools

Email your local school and ask about their interest in starting a chapter. This step can take time, so be patient.

Step 5: Start the club!

Once you have the necessary approvals and support, you can officially start teaching afterschool clubs at your selected elementary school.

We are always in need of chaperones

We sometimes have to reduce club sizes or cancel sessions due to a lack of adult supervision. If you are a parent or guardian interested in helping out, please sign up to be a chaperone.

The process is different for every school, so please consult your local school's website for more information.

Most of the time, chaperones are responsible for attendance. During lessons, you are free to help or observe as long as you stay in the classroom.

*Most schools require adult volunteers to complete a background check.
